Your Journey

How It Works

From enrollment to placement — here's what your 12-week journey looks like.

01

Pick Your Track & Enroll

Choose one of 5 role-based tracks. Enroll online as an individual student, or join through your partner college for an on-campus experience.

02

Join Your Professional Company

You're assigned to a cross-functional team — just like a real startup. Software Engineers, QA, Data Analysts, HR, and Marketing all work together.

03

Build Real Projects in Sprints

Work in 2-week Scrum sprints. Pick Jira tickets, push code to Git, run automated tests, build dashboards, and present sprint demos — all real.

04

Get Expert Feedback Every Week

Industry-experienced mentors review your work, provide code reviews, and guide your growth with weekly 1-on-1 feedback sessions.

05

Graduate With a Portfolio

Week 12 ends with a company-wide demo. You leave with a GitHub portfolio, QA reports, dashboards, and marketing decks — ready for interviews.

06

Placement Assistance (Months 3–6)

Active placement support for 3 months after the program. Maintain 70% attendance + 50% grade to qualify. We prep you and connect you with employers. Placements are facilitated, not guaranteed.
