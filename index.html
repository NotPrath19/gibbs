How It Works

Our Delivery Model

We don't just teach — we embed. Our instructors work on your campus, with your students, for 3 full months.

01

We Deploy Instructors to Your Campus

Experienced Gibbs Edu Tech instructors arrive at your college and set up the professional company environment. No infrastructure changes needed from your end.

02

Students Choose Their Role Track

Students select one of the 5 tracks: Software Engineer, QA Engineer, Data Analyst, HR Specialist, or Marketing Associate. 2 batches of 60 students per program.

03

Professional Company Kicks Off

All 5 roles work together as a real startup team — building, testing, analyzing, recruiting, and marketing a real web application over 12 weeks. Scrum ceremonies, Jira boards, Git repos, and Docker deployments — all real.

04

Weekly Progress & Mentorship

Each week has structured goals, deliverables, and mentor feedback. Students present sprint demos, QA release reports, data insights, HR summaries, and marketing campaigns.

05

Final Demo & Portfolio

Week 12 culminates in a company-wide demo where students present their work — with GitHub portfolios, QA reports, dashboards, HR summaries, and marketing decks ready for job interviews.
