Why This Program

You won't just watch lectures. You'll work as a professional inside a simulated company — with real responsibilities, real deadlines, and real teammates across multiple departments.

Professional Company Environment

Work inside a simulated company with structured teams and workflows. Whether you're writing code, running tests, analyzing data, recruiting talent, or planning campaigns — you'll experience what a real job feels like.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Software Engineers, QA, Data Analysts, HR & Marketing — all 5 roles work together on one product. You'll collaborate across departments, just like in a real company.

Industry Tools & Agentic AI

Your Journey

How It Works

From enrollment to employment opportunities — here's what your journey looks like.

01

Pick Your Track & Enroll

Choose one of 5 role-based tracks. Enroll online as an individual student, or join through your partner college for an on-campus experience.

02

Join Your Professional Company

You're assigned to a cross-functional team — just like a real company. Software Engineers, QA, Data Analysts, HR, and Marketing all work together on the same product.

03

Learn, Build & Deliver

Follow a structured 12-week curriculum for your track. Apply what you learn immediately by working on real deliverables — code, test reports, dashboards, HR documents, or marketing campaigns.

04

Get Regular Feedback

Industry-experienced mentors review your work and guide your progress with structured evaluations and actionable feedback throughout the program.

05

Graduate With a Portfolio

Week 12 ends with a final presentation. You leave with real work artifacts — code repos, test suites, analytics dashboards, HR playbooks, or campaign reports — ready for job interviews.

06

Employment Opportunities (Months 3–6)

We provide interview opportunities with hiring companies for 3 months after the program. You crack the interview, you get the job. Maintain 70% attendance + 50% grade to qualify. Placements are not guaranteed — you earn your position through the interview.
